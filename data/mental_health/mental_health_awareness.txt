
TITLE: Mental Health Awareness
SOURCE: World Health Organization
REFERENCE: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/mental-health-strengthening-our-response

Mental health is influenced by individual, social, and structural factors.

Mental health promotion and prevention involve actions that support individuals and communities.

People should not be judged or stereotyped because of mental health concerns.

Reliable information and access to appropriate services are important.
