
TITLE: Anxiety
SOURCE: National Institute of Mental Health
REFERENCE: https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/anxiety-disorders

Anxiety is a normal response to stress. It can help people recognize challenges. Anxiety disorders involve intense or persistent anxiety that may affect daily activities.

Different anxiety disorders have different characteristics. Only qualified health professionals can assess an individual and determine whether they have an anxiety disorder.

This document provides general educational information, not diagnosis.
