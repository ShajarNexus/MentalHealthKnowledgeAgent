
TITLE: Common Mental Health Terminology
SOURCE: National Institute of Mental Health
REFERENCE: https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health

Mental health describes emotional, psychological, and social wellbeing.

A symptom is an experience or change that may be associated with a health condition.

A diagnosis is a clinical determination made by an appropriately qualified professional.

Stress, anxiety, depression, symptoms, and disorders do not all mean the same thing.
