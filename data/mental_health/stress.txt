
TITLE: Understanding Stress
SOURCE: World Health Organization
REFERENCE: https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/stress

Stress is a natural human response to difficult or demanding situations.

It can affect how people feel, think, and behave. People may experience stress differently.

Recognizing stress, maintaining helpful routines, connecting with others, and using healthy coping skills can support wellbeing.
